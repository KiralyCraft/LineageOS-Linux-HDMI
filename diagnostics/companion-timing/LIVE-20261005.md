## First bounded HDMI sessions

Two timing sessions reached `TIMING_VALID` (generations 9 and 10) before the
broker delivered their leases to Xorg. The second was inspected while active:
DP-1/CRTC 235, 3840x2160 at 60 Hz, FD740 glamor, TearFree enabled and
`HDMI_LOS_XORG_ASYNC_ABI=1`. Native-fence readiness drove real flips, and the
USB mouse and keyboard were grabbed by the input bridge. The user confirmed
that the desktop and USB input worked, followed by return to Android at timeout.

Forty-two aggregated summaries in the second session covered 332 copies.
Submission/export averaged 1.547 ms and fence-to-callback delay averaged 3.887
ms, with maxima 18.039 and 34.324 ms respectively. The latter includes event-loop
dispatch latency. This was an uncontrolled desktop/startup workload; it does
not establish a performance improvement over the previous moving-window tests,
and neither copy count nor callback rate is physical displayed FPS.

Both timeout paths restored Android and closed the companion session FD; module
use count returned to zero and kernel taint stayed 12804. However Xorg reported
`HDMI asynchronous TearFree flip submission failed` during timeout restoration.
The broker's shared startup/stop wait used the already-expired session deadline,
so it skipped waiting for the agent to finish before revoking the lease. Its
two-second stop allowance was also shorter than the agent's maximum sequence of
three two-second child shutdowns. This prevents claiming a clean shutdown gate.

Commit `fadb97b` gives teardown its own eight-second acknowledgement budget,
ignores startup escape/deadline checks during that wait, and drains final readable
acknowledgements before treating HUP as failure. Timing-guard sessions renew the
composer crash watchdog independently of the user's 60-second session policy,
providing shutdown headroom. The kernel module and Xorg binaries are unchanged.
Broker tests exercise an expired startup deadline versus a valid stop ACK,
held volume escape, final ACK plus HUP, closed peers and a bounded missing ACK.
All 48 source tests and native checks passed. **The revised broker still needs
another live bounded shutdown test.**

The revision logs initial timing metadata and final get/put counters from the
session FD before closing it. Earlier use-count observations establish that the
FD closed; they do not independently prove the vblank get/put balance. The live
read-only attempt to duplicate the second session FD ran after timeout and
returned EBADF, producing no additional timing measurement.
